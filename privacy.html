Privacy Policy

TL;DR: We don't collect personal data. We don't track you. We don't sell anything.

Our Commitment

At cili.xyz, we believe privacy is a fundamental right. Our service is designed with privacy as a core principle, not an afterthought.

Data We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide our service:

  • Search Queries: Temporarily processed to return results. Not permanently stored or linked to any user.
  • Language Preference: Stored locally in your browser (localStorage) to remember your language choice.
  • Cached Data: Search results may be cached temporarily to improve performance for all users.

What We Don't Collect

We explicitly do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, etc.)
  • IP addresses for tracking purposes
  • Browsing history or download history
  • Device fingerprints
  • Location data

Cookies

We use only essential browser storage:

  • localStorage: To store your language preference
  • sessionStorage: To temporarily cache search results for faster navigation

We do NOT use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or any third-party cookies.

Third-Party Services

We use minimal third-party services:

  • Cloudflare: For hosting and CDN services. Cloudflare may collect basic access logs for security purposes.
  • Flag Icons CDN: For displaying country flag icons. No tracking involved.

Data Security

Since we don't collect personal data, there's nothing to breach. Our infrastructure is designed to:

  • Use HTTPS encryption for all connections
  • Not log user activities
  • Not create user profiles or accounts

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
