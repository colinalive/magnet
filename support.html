Frequently Asked Questions

What is cili.xyz?

cili.xyz (Magnet Pioneer) is a search engine for discovering torrent metadata. We index publicly available information from across the internet to help you find what you're looking for.

Do you host any files?

No. We are a search engine only. We do not host, store, or distribute any files or torrent content. We only index metadata (titles, sizes, hashes) that is already publicly available.

What is a magnet link?

A magnet link is a type of hyperlink that allows you to download files using a BitTorrent client. It contains a hash that uniquely identifies the content, rather than a specific file location.

How do I use a magnet link?

Click the "Copy Magnet Link" button on any detail page, then paste the link into your preferred BitTorrent client (such as qBittorrent, Transmission, or uTorrent).

Why isn't my search returning results?

Try using different keywords or check your spelling. Our search works best with specific terms. If you're searching in a non-English language, make sure your search terms are spelled correctly.

What do Seeds and Leechers mean?

Seeds are users who have the complete file and are sharing it. Leechers are users who are currently downloading. More seeds generally means faster download speeds.

Is it legal to use cili.xyz?

cili.xyz is a search engine, similar to Google. Searching for information is legal. However, downloading copyrighted content without permission may be illegal in your jurisdiction. Always respect copyright laws.

How can I change the language?

Click on the language selector in the top right corner of any page. We support English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.
