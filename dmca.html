DMCA Policy

cili.xyz respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects its users to do the same. In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998 ("DMCA"), we will respond expeditiously to claims of copyright infringement.

What We Are

cili.xyz is a search engine that indexes publicly available torrent metadata from various sources across the internet. We do not host any files, torrents, or copyrighted content on our servers. We simply provide a search interface to help users find information that is already publicly available.

No File Hosting

We want to make it absolutely clear that:

  • We do NOT host any torrent files or copyrighted material
  • We do NOT track or store any user downloads
  • We only index metadata (titles, sizes, hashes) that is publicly available
  • Magnet links are hash-based references, not hosted content

Takedown Requests

If you are a copyright owner or authorized agent and believe that content indexed by our search engine infringes your copyright, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice. Please include:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
  • The exact URL(s) on cili.xyz where the allegedly infringing material appears
  • Your contact information (name, address, phone, email)
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate
  • Your physical or electronic signature

Contact Information

Email: dmca@cili.xyz

Response Time: We aim to respond within 24-48 hours

Our Process

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will:

  • Review the request for completeness and validity
  • Remove the search result from our index if appropriate
  • Notify you of the action taken

Counter-Notification

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notification with the required information as specified in the DMCA.
